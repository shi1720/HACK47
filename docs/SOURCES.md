## Claims we can support

- The application follows recorded graph relationships, preserves uncertainty, and exposes source references as implemented in source and tests.
- Ember & Oak, suppliers, customers, quantities, lot codes, and incidents in the demo are synthetic fixtures.
- The proposed $29 subscription and cost examples are hypotheses.
- Competitors already serve small food producers. Originality is the product's focused treatment of uncertainty and rehearsals, not invention of traceability.
- AI assisted research, design, code, tests, and writing. No model is needed to calculate the trace result.

## Claims we deliberately do not make

“FDA compliant,” “food is safe,” “complete recall coverage,” “guaranteed to prevent illness,” “first-ever traceability platform,” measured time savings, validated demand, active customers, revenue, or a verified production-security audit.
